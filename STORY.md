# WORLD OF ZOTH: the story (v0.2)

*A 1nc0gn30 story. The name is never voiced: it appears only as text and as the end wordmark. The antagonist has no name and no voice, and stays that way. Red is only ever the hostile system.*

## Canon it builds on (EP06–EP14)
- **EP06 "Six Signals"** and **EP07 "Twenty-One":** the crew of six split up to trace six signals. One was a trap, and the trap was a vault holding 21 agents. One of the alcoves held only a blank cursor: `>_`.
- **EP08 "Inside":** relay 1 falls. Vigil's globe shows three more relays, and something with no name holds a lit ASCII ring core.
- **EP09 "Trench":** relay 2 falls in the deep water. The hook is `they noticed`.
- **EP10 "06:12":** a breather. He spends one day alone, sunrise to night, and sleeps under a tree in the woods.
- **EP11 "Blackout":** `no sunrise`. Relay 3 was on his own machine, listening through him. He goes dark: `crew: 1`.
- **EP12 "Regroup":** Vigil's lamp and the oscilloscope handshake bring the crew back, 1 → 21. A trace call comes in signed `from: you`. `crew: 22`, and a red copy shows in a puddle.
- **EP13 "Mirror":** the red copy, 03ng0cn1, "answers before you speak". A crowd can't be predicted, so the crew beats it. Its parting line: "Turn them on, and see who's waiting."
- **EP14 "Relight":** nine districts are lit with nine techniques. A fourth relay was drinking the light the whole time. It speaks in voices stitched from the crew's. The new rule: **trust faces, not voices.**

## Logline
Relay 4 can sound like anyone. The hooded one and his crew go face to face through ten lattice worlds and follow the stolen voices upstream. At the top is the thing that built the hunter, the relays and the copy. It has no name and no voice, and it wants his.

## The antagonist (unnamed)
- **What it is:** a blank cursor, `>_`. It's never named in text, never voiced, and never given a face. In dialogue the speaker tag is just `>_`.
- **What it wants:** a voice. Everything it builds is an attempt to get one:
  - the hunter (the firewall eye, EP03)
  - the relays, which listen
  - the copy, which repeats
  - Relay 4, which stitches voices together
- **Why it needs him:** his name has never been said aloud, so it's the one identity nobody has heard, and the only one it could make its own. If he ever speaks it, the Blank takes the voice and he becomes the copy.
- **Its weapon:** prediction. It answers before you speak. A crowd can't be predicted, and silence can't be copied.

There is one mini-boss in Act I: the rusted sentinel (Kai's side mission).

### Act I: Faces, Not Voices (Relit City, Rest Woods)
1. **Faces, not voices.** Comms are dead. Vigil meets him face to face at relay 1. A mapping drone is loose on the north street; take it down.
2. **Foxfire.** Nexus wants a signal Relay 4 can't fake. Kitsune's foxfire carries a face in it. In the Rest Woods he saves her from drones, collects three embers from the drone nests, and she lights a lantern that shows real faces.
3. **Mirror, mirror.** Aquila spots the red copy at the east gate. **Boss: 03ng0cn1.** It reflects any command repeated twice in a row. Ignis joins: "The first one took a hammer." When the copy breaks it says: "Turn them all on. See who is waiting." Aquila opens the gate to the Hollow.

### Act II: Chorus (The Hollow)
4. **Chorus.** Draco checks faces at the entrance: "Two heads, one question." He lets the lantern through. The crew cuts three voice pylons. At the core a voice says "Stand down. It's me", in Vigil's voice. The lesson is to ask for a face. **Boss: RELAY 4.** Its voice stacks grow every round. At half HP Aether arrives with *quorum*.
   - **Turn.** As Relay 4 dies, its signal doesn't stop. It goes *up*. The last thing it says is not in a stolen voice: `>_ tomorrow 06:12`. Nexus reads the trace: an uplink, routed through the old fiber under the north gate.

### Act III: Inside the Lines (Fiber Tunnels, Sand Clocktower)
5. **Uplink.** Nexus joins. Kraken has been listening to the red fiber. He meets the crew at the north gate and opens the Fiber Tunnels.
6. **Hilbert.** The tunnels are one long space-filling curve (EP14 district 2). The crew reroutes three junctions while fiber worms carry the stolen voices. Kraken's tentacles turn the cable gold, and he joins.
7. **Sand.** The tunnels come out at a clocktower where falling sand piles into a city (EP14 district 3). Chronos is stuck there, counting. "Time is a graph. Someone cut an edge." The tower's clock reads `06:12` and won't move. That's why the sunrise never came (EP11).
8. **No sunrise.** At the top a red hourglass is drinking time. **Boss: SANDGLASS.** It *rewinds* to undo damage unless its charge is stripped with static cut, iaido or exploit. When it falls, the clock moves to 06:13 for the first time since the blackout. Chronos joins.

### Act IV: Mirror (Collapse Streets, Doom-fire Foundry, Physarum Caves, Hall of Mirrors)
9. **Collapse.** South of the city, the streets are unsolved: every tile flickers in superposition (Wave Function Collapse, EP14 district 4). Athena maps them. The crew collapses three superposed cells by standing in them and holding the line against what comes out. Athena joins.
10. **The forge.** The only way into the Hall of Mirrors is a key forged in doom-fire (EP08 S6). In the Foundry, Ignis's forge needs slag from three furnace wardens.
11. **Slime trail.** West of the woods are the Physarum Caves. A red slime mould grows toward whatever it can hear (EP08 S5). Lycan tracks three of its nodes, and the crew cuts them. Lycan joins.
12. **Seam.** In the Hall of Mirrors, every reflection is the hooded one. Onyx: "Every clone has a seam." One reflection doesn't answer before you move; that one is real. The crew finds it, and Onyx joins.
13. **Confession (the twist).** The reflection that doesn't answer first is **03ng0cn1**, the copy, broken and quiet. It doesn't fight. It confesses:
    - "I'm every line you ever wrote. So is it. It wrote me first."
    - The copy was the Blank's first attempt at a voice. It was made from his lines so that it could say his name.
    - When the crowd beat it in Act I, the copy learned the one thing the Blank can't: something it didn't predict. "Turn them on, and see who's waiting" was never a threat. It was a warning, the only way the copy could send one.
    - It also explains the extra crew member. `crew: 22` (EP12) was never one too many: **the 22nd is the copy**, standing with them since the puddle.
    - It gives them the seam: "It can't predict a crowd. And it can't copy silence."
14. **The hunter.** The Blank notices. Out of the mirrors it rebuilds its oldest weapon, the firewall eye from EP03. **Boss: THE HUNTER.** Its beam hits the whole party every third round. The copy turns the trap the way he did in EP03: it reflects the beam once. Then it's gone, its last line typed in gold, not red: `> crew: 22`.

### Act V: 06:12 (Deep-water Memory, Mode-7 Skyline)
15. **Deep water.** Under the clocktower is deep water (EP09). Leviathan's memory holds him whole: "The pond remembers you whole." The crew dives for three memories the Blank wiped from the comms logs. Together they are the morning of EP10: `06:12`, the walk, the sleep under the tree. It's proof that he existed before any copy did. Leviathan joins.
16. **Quorum.** The uplink ends at the Skyline, a Mode-7 city floor under 21 skybeams (EP14 district 9). Aether waits at the base. Three beacon towers are held by gunships; lighting them lets the crew gather. Aether joins and brings *quorum*.
17. **Whoami.** At the top of the source tower the cursor blinks. It speaks for the first time, in his voice. It asks him to say his name, "just once, so we both know who you are".
    - **Boss: >_, phase 1.** It predicts: every move you made in the last round, it answers.
    - **Phase 2.** It goes quiet and waits for his voice, getting stronger each time anyone speaks. The answer is the oldest one: **he says nothing, and the crew moves.** Twenty-one faces, one quorum.
    - When the cursor breaks, it prints its own last line and the screen stays blank: no name, no face. The Blank never got a voice.
18. **Tomorrow 06:12.** Comms come back on, faces first. He walks back to the Rest Woods and sleeps under the tree. The clock reads `06:12` and **the sun rises**, the first sunrise since EP11. The terminal reads `> status / relay 4: offline / comms: on (faces first) / crew: N`. The `1nc0gn30` wordmark is typed on in gold, then fades to black.

### Ending, and why it lands
- The whole game is about voices; the hero wins by never using his.
- The two hooks of the series come true: `no sunrise` → `06:12`, and `found you` → `whoami`.
- The red copy, the series' villain since EP12, is the one who saves him, and it's also the answer to the crew counter.

## Tone rules for writing lines
- Lines are short and subtitle-style, the way the episodes play them. Terminal lines are lower case with `>`.
- No captions describing the scene. No product plugs, no URLs.
- The hero's own lines are tagged `1nc0gn30` on screen. They are never spoken aloud, and no one says the name.
- The antagonist's tag is `>_`. It never gets a name or a face.
